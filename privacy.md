# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 29/09/2026**

## 1. Who We Are

iVentPad ("we", "us", "our") is a social event planning application that helps users discover events, connect with friends, co-plan gatherings, and communicate in real time. We are based in Kampala, Uganda, and operate globally.

**Data Controller:** iVentPad
**Contact:** katedodvick@gnail.com
**Website:** https://github.com/Kate-Dodvick/iventpad-legal

## 2. Summary of What We Do

We collect only the information needed to run the app. We do not sell your data. We do not show third-party ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide

| Data | When We Collect It | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Sign-up | To identify you to others |
| Email address | Sign-up | Authentication, password reset, important notices |
| Username | Sign-up | Public handle in the app |
| Password (encrypted) | Sign-up | Authentication — we never see your raw password |
| Profile photo | Optional | Displayed on your profile and messages |
| Bio | Optional | Displayed on your profile |
| Event content | When you create events | Title, venue, date, description, cover photo, price, categories |
| Chat messages | When you send messages | Delivered to the intended recipients |
| Feedback | Optional | To improve the service |

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Last seen timestamp | When you use the app | To show others when you were last active |
| Read receipts | When you open a chat | To show delivery/read status |
| Approximate location | Only when you explicitly request nearby places | To show relevant places and events |
| Device type & OS version | Automatically | For compatibility and bug fixes |
| App version | Automatically | To know which build you're running |
| Error logs | Automatically | To detect and fix crashes |

**We do NOT collect:** your exact GPS coordinates continuously, your contact list, your photos outside of what you upload, your phone number, your payment information (we do not process payments at this time), your browsing history in other apps.

### 3.3 Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your payment card details
- We do not access your camera or microphone
- We do not read your SMS or call logs
- We do not use advertising identifiers
- We do not use third-party analytics trackers

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

- Provide and operate the app
- Authenticate you
- Show your profile to other users as intended
- Deliver messages, event invitations, and notifications
- Show your last-seen status to others
- Enable you to discover events and places
- Improve the app based on anonymised usage patterns
- Respond to support requests and reports
- Comply with legal obligations

## 5. How We Share Your Information

**We do not sell your personal data.**

We share data only in these cases:

| Recipient | What We Share | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Other users | Your name, username, profile photo, bio, event content you post, messages you send, last-seen status | This is the core function of the app |
| Supabase | All data stored in the app | Our database, authentication, and storage provider |
| Expo / EAS | Device info, build info | App distribution and updates |
| Google Play | App usage metadata | Distribution of the app |
| Law enforcement | Only when legally required | Compliance with valid legal requests |

We do not use any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## 6. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase uses industry-standard security and may store data in data centres located in the United States, Europe, or other regions. By using iVentPad, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations.

## 7. How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data (name, email, username, profile) | Until you delete your account |
| Event content | Until you delete the event or your account |
| Chat messages | Until you delete the message or your account |
| Feedback | 24 months from submission |
| Crash/error logs | 90 days |
| Last-seen data | Continuously updated; removed on account deletion |

When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — contact us to receive a copy
- **Correct** your data — edit your profile in the app at any time
- **Delete** your data — see Section 9 below
- **Withdraw consent** — stop using the app at any time
- **Complain** — contact your local data protection authority

## 9. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time using either method:

### In the app:
1. Open the app and sign in
2. Go to your Profile
3. Tap "Delete Account"
4. Confirm deletion

### On the web:
Visit **[your-deletion-page-url]** and submit a deletion request. We will process it within 30 days.

When your account is deleted:
- Your profile, events, messages, and personal data are permanently removed
- Your interactions with other users may remain visible to those users in anonymised form (e.g., "a deleted user")
- You will not be able to recover your account

## 10. Children's Privacy

iVentPad is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We use industry-standard security measures:
- All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Passwords are hashed and salted by Supabase Auth
- Access is restricted by row-level security policies
- Only authorised personnel (currently: the founder) can access the database

No system is 100% secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to katedodvick@gmail.com.
